## 앱 이름
- **Symptomly** (표시명)

## 짧은 설명 (~80자)
> Private symptom & medication diary for chronic illness — export a clear summary for your doctor.

## 전체 설명 (필수 키워드 포함)
Symptomly is a **private, local-first symptom tracker** for people living with **chronic illness** (lupus, Crohn's disease, fibromyalgia, and more).

- **Log symptoms** with severity, notes, photos, and the hospital you visited
- **Track medications** — doses, pharmacy, prescription photos, and a daily **medication schedule** with reminders
- **Log mood, sleep, and food** to spot patterns
- **Understand your data** — symptom trends, monthly chart, heatmap, medication vs symptom correlation, medication adherence
- **Be ready for your doctor** — export a clean CSV or **printable PDF summary** to bring to your appointment
- **Your data stays on your device** — no account, no cloud, no tracking. Optional PIN encryption.

Symptomly is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency advice.

## 스크린샷 계획 (5장)
1. 홈 대시보드 — 히어로 스탯 + 트렌드 차트 + 오늘 복약
2. 기록 화면 — 증상 다중 추가 + 자동완성
3. 복약 — 스케줄 + 복용 체크 + 순응도
4. 통계 — 월별 트렌드 차트 + 히트맵
5. 의사 공유 — 요약 + PDF 내보내기
