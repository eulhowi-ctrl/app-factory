## 제목
**"I Showed My Doctor a Chart — Not a Phone Full of Screenshots"**

A chronic-illness patient's simple tool: track symptoms privately, walk into the appointment with a summary.

## 본문 (요약)

If you live with a chronic condition — lupus, Crohn's, fibromyalgia — you've been here: the doctor asks
"how have you been feeling?" and you fumble through three months of memory.

Symptomly is a **private, local-first diary** built for that moment.

- Everything stays **on your device**. No account, no cloud, no ads selling your health data.
- Log **symptoms, medications, mood, sleep, and food** in a few taps.
- See **trends**: symptom frequency, monthly chart, a calendar heatmap, and whether a medication seems to help.
- Before your visit, **export a one-page summary** — CSV or print-to-PDF — and hand it to your doctor.

It's not a diagnosis tool. It's a record you own.
