Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

1. Data we collect

Symptomly does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter (symptoms, medications, notes) is stored locally on your device only. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no servers.

2. Advertising

The Android version may display ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Where required (EEA/UK), a consent screen is shown before personalized ads. Purchasing the premium upgrade removes ads.

3. Purchases

The premium upgrade (ad removal + statistics) is a one-time in-app purchase processed by the Google Play Store. No payment information is handled by Symptomly.

4. Medical disclaimer

Symptomly is a record-keeping tool only. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.
